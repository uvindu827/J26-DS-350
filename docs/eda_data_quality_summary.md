## Lag relationships with rainfall and runoff (training years 1997–2017)
Method: within each cell, the seasonal cycle was removed from every variable, and Spearman correlation
was calculated between the nutrient on day t and the driver on day t − L (L = 0–30). Results were then
summarised across cells (median and range); cells were never pooled. Descriptive only, without p-values,
because daily series are autocorrelated.

- **Nitrate** – Western: strongest with surface runoff, median ρ = +0.16 at 0 days (cells +0.06 to +0.17); Southern: negligible for all three drivers (all |median ρ| < 0.1 over 0–30 days).
- **Phosphate** – Western: negligible for all three drivers (all |median ρ| < 0.1 over 0–30 days); Southern: negligible for all three drivers (all |median ρ| < 0.1 over 0–30 days).
- **Silicate** – Western: strongest with sub-surface runoff, median ρ = -0.15 at 26 days (cells -0.19 to -0.14); Southern: strongest with sub-surface runoff, median ρ = -0.22 at 22 days (cells -0.26 to -0.17).

Without removing the seasonal cycle, correlations are larger because nutrients and rainfall share the
monsoon calendar (`rho_raw_seasonal_median` in `E36_lag_correlations.csv`). Figure: `E36_lag_correlations.png`.

**Feature windows.** The existing 3-, 7-, 14- and 30-day rainfall and runoff windows were selected a priori in Step 06 from hydrological reasoning (storm response within days, then weekly, two-week and monthly catchment memory, following the ML-SWAN-inspired design), before any model was trained. This lag analysis is supporting, descriptive evidence only. It was not used to select, add or remove features, and no model was retrained.

**Causation.** Correlation is not causation. These correlations describe association between reanalysis forcing and model-derived nutrient fields; they do not show that rainfall or runoff causes nutrient changes.

## Limitations
- Targets are model-derived hindcast fields, not field measurements; forcing is reanalysis.
- Each cell is a 0.25° (~28 km) grid square; three outer cells take land forcing from their nearest land points.
- Correlations are descriptive associations for training years only.
