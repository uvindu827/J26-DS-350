# Component 1 – per-season evaluation (PP1)

Generated by `scripts/16_season_evaluation.py`. Read-only and inference only: the six saved neural runs
were loaded and evaluated with gradients disabled; nothing was retrained, tuned or re-selected.
**Automatic checks: 36 of 36 passed**, including exact reproduction of every E23 test
RMSE and of the Component 2 export (`evidence/pp1/E37_season_evaluation_log.txt`).

## How seasons were evaluated
- Rows: the same 244,272 common validation + test rows as the fair comparison (E23); the
  headline uses the 122,136 **held-out test rows (2022–2025)**. Validation is kept in E38 as a reference.
- Season = calendar month of the **forecast target date**.
- Neural models: metrics computed for each of 3 seeds, then averaged (spread shown as whiskers); no ensemble,
  no seed selection.

## Interpretation limits
- Seasons are fixed calendar-month labels assigned by the forecast target date (NEM Dec–Feb, FIM Mar–Apr, SWM May–Sep, SIM Oct–Nov). They are descriptive groupings, not measured monsoon onset or withdrawal dates.
- The actual year-to-year environmental variability (rainfall, runoff, soil moisture, wind) enters the models through ERA5 / ERA5-Land reanalysis forcing, not through the season label alone.
- Results describe forecast performance during each season. They do not show that the monsoon causes any difference, and they do not show that any model predicts or captures monsoon onset.
- Targets are Copernicus Marine model-derived hindcast fields (no data assimilation), not field or in-situ measurements. Evaluation is in hindcast-forcing (replay) mode.
- No model, seed or setting was chosen from these seasonal test results.
